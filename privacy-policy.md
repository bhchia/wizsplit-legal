# WizSplit: Split & Track Bills — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 10 November 2026

This policy explains what information WizSplit: Split & Track Bills ("WizSplit", "the app") uses, why, who receives it and the choices you have. WizSplit is made by **bitwigblazer**, the developer of WizSplit on Google Play ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the information described here.

WizSplit is meant to be used around the world. We follow the strictest of the rules that apply to it, including the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and other US state privacy laws, Brazil's LGPD, Canada's PIPEDA, Japan's APPI, Singapore's PDPA, Australia's Privacy Act, South Korea's PIPA and India's DPDP Act. Where a law gives you more rights than this policy describes, you have those rights.

## In short

- **No account, no sign-in.** We don't ask for your email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
- **Your bills stay on your phone.** Amounts, names of people, items and settings are saved only on your device. We have no server that stores them.
- **No ads** in this version, and no advertising ID is used.
- **Crash reports** help us fix bugs. They contain technical details about the crash, not your bills.
- We **don't sell** your personal information, and we don't track you.

## 1. Information kept only on your phone

To let you come back to a bill, the app saves on your phone: the bills you split (their amounts, currency, charges, items, the names you type for people and for the bill, who paid and who is marked as paid), recent names to suggest, your last-used currency, and settings such as light or dark theme. The app also reads, on the phone only, the phone's language and region (to suggest a currency).

The names you type may be the real names of other people, such as friends or colleagues. They are kept only on your phone and are not sent to us.

This information is **not sent to us**. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. You can also delete a bill inside the app. If you use Android's backup, Android may include the app's data in your own Google account backup, under Google's terms; you can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

## 2. Sharing a bill

When you tap **Share**, the app prepares a text summary of the split and hands it to the app you choose (for example, a messaging app). What you share, and with whom, is your choice. After you share, the text is handled by that app under its own terms and privacy policy, not by us. Check the names and amounts before sending.

## 3. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes, **Firebase Crashlytics** (a Google service) sends us a crash report: the error and where in the code it happened, device model, operating system version, app version, the time, and a random installation identifier (not your name or advertising ID). We use crash reports only to find and fix bugs. Crash reports are kept for 90 days. We don't use Google Analytics. Your bills, names and amounts are not part of a crash report.

**Legal basis (GDPR):** our legitimate interest in keeping the app working. See Firebase's privacy information: <https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy>

## 4. Advertising

This version of WizSplit contains no ads and no advertising software. If a later version adds ads, we will update this policy first, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

## 5. Who receives information

Only Google (Firebase Crashlytics), for the purpose described in section 3, and the app you choose when you share a bill (section 2). We don't sell personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

## 6. International transfers

Google may process crash reports in the United States and other countries. Google relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers from the EEA, UK and Switzerland.

## 7. How long information is kept

- On your phone: until you delete it, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
- Crash reports: 90 days.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information; to object to or restrict its use; to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting earlier use); and not to be treated differently for using these rights.

Because we keep no account and no server copy of your bills, you can remove them yourself: delete a bill in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall it. For anything else, email us at **bitwigblazer@gmail.com**. We will reply within the time the law requires (usually 30 days). You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

## 9. Children

WizSplit is a general tool for adults and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the age set by local law, such as 16 in some EU countries). If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete what we can.

## 10. Security

Information on your phone is protected by Android's app storage, which other apps can't read. Crash reports travel over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

## 11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app or on the app's store page.
