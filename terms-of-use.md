WizSplit is a free app for working out how to split a bill between people: equally, by items or by exact amounts, with service charge, tax, discount and tip. ## 2. Results are guides, not guarantees

We work to make the app accurate, and the shares it shows always add up to the bill you entered. But please check anything important before you ask someone to pay:

- **Calculations depend on what you enter.** A wrong amount, item or person gives a wrong split.
- **Service charge, tax and tip values** are typical suggestions for a currency or country. Rates and customs vary by place and change over time; the venue's own bill always applies.
- **Rounding.** Amounts are rounded to the currency's smallest unit; when a bill doesn't divide exactly, a few people may pay one unit more or less than others.
- **"Paid" marks are only notes.** Marking someone as paid records a note on your phone. WizSplit does not hold, send, collect or move money, does not check any payment, and does not tell anyone that a payment was made.
